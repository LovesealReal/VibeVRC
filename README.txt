Thank you for downloading VibeVRC!
Control your partner's toy from your VRChat avatar menu, from anywhere.

HOW TO INSTALL:

- Put VibeVRC.exe in its own folder first. It saves its settings next to itself
- Open VibeVRC.exe (if Windows warns you, click More info > Run anyway)
- If Windows asks about network access, click Allow. That's how VRChat finds VibeVRC
- Pick who has the toy

SHARING YOUR TOY:

- Pick "I do" when it asks who has the toy
- Turn on Bluetooth and your toy, and close Lovense Remote. VibeVRC finds the toy by itself
- Press Copy and send your share code to your partner
- Press Pause any time to stop everything
- If the toy connects better to your phone, pick "My phone" and follow the steps there
- If Find my phone doesn't work, type the Server Address shown in Intiface on your phone (like 192.168.1.23:12345) and press Use
- Only use "My phone" on Wi-Fi you trust, like your home Wi-Fi

CONTROLLING SOMEONE:

- Pick "They do" when it asks who has the toy
- Turn on OSC in VRChat (Action Menu > Options > OSC > Enabled)
- In the Control tab, add their name and the share code they sent you
- Press Test to check it works
- Use the VibeVRC menu on your avatar. Target 1 is the first person in your list
- To control your own toy from your avatar menu, add yourself with your own share code. It works without sharing turned on

AVATAR HAPTICS:

- Wear an avatar with SPS (from VRCFury) and turn on OSC in VRChat
- Go to the Haptics tab and turn on "My avatar controls my toy"
- The deeper something goes in, the stronger it buzzes. Hands and touch zones count too
- Strength sets how strong touches feel. Intensity and Pattern in the avatar menu are only for Vibrate
- Strokers like the Solace Pro move with how deep things go. If it moves the wrong way, turn on "Flip direction"
- Turn on "Send my touches" to send your avatar's touches to whoever you picked under "Target"
- Close any other haptics app first. Two apps can't share a toy

NOTES:

- Keep VibeVRC open while you play. Closing it stops everything
- Pause and Stop sending stay on until you turn them off, even if you close VibeVRC
- Toys that rotate, like the Nora, spin along with the vibration
- "Connected" at the top means you're online and commands can go through
- Works alongside other OSC apps like VRCFT
- Your settings and share code are saved in a VibeVRC Data folder next to VibeVRC.exe, so keep them together
- If something goes wrong, vibevrc.log in that folder has what happened (you can also press Copy log in the Log tab)
- Don't send anyone share_code.txt or settings.json. They have your codes in them
